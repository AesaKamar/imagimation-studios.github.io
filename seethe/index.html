What can I do with Seethe?

Explore and evolve fractal flames, discover variations, zoom and pan, pause to refine the image, and share a link to the same flame. Supported native versions also include ambient sound and a local Shares history. Features vary by platform.

Can I try Seethe in a browser?

Yes. The web viewer lets you explore a flame without installing the app. The interactive field guide explains how fractal flames work.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. Creating or opening shared flame links needs an internet connection. Anyone with a shared link can open it.
