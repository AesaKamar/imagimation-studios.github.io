Privacy requests

Remove a share.
Manage your data.

Seethe has no user account. Local share history and public flame links are separate, so removing one does not automatically remove the other.

Remove local share history

  1. Open Seethe and choose Discover → Shares.
  2. Use the remove control beside the entry you want to delete.
  3. Repeat for any other entries. The list stores no more than 25 flames.

On Android, clearing app storage removes local app data. On iPhone and iPad, delete the app rather than offloading it if you want its local data removed. On Mac, removing the application alone may leave preferences; use the in-app controls to remove share-history entries, or contact support for help. Device backups are managed separately by your platform provider.

Request removal of a public flame

Email contact@imagimationstudios.org with:

  • The full Seethe sharing URL.
  • A brief explanation of what you want removed and your connection to it.
Request share removal

We may ask for reasonable verification before acting. We will assess the request under applicable law. Removing a live record stops the service from supplying that share after caches refresh; it does not erase copies already saved by other people or social platforms. Provider backups and logs may remain for their retention periods.

Other personal-information requests

Use the same contact address for access, correction, or deletion requests relating to support messages or other personal information. Include enough context for us to locate the information, but do not send passwords or identity documents unless we specifically explain why verification is needed.
