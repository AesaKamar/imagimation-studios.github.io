About the app

A little universe.
A different pace.

Seethe is an evolving fractal art experience from ImagiMation Studios LLC. It turns mathematical transformations into moving color, texture, and light.

Each flame is a set of mathematical instructions. Small changes can reveal a spiral, a cloud, a delicate structure, or something you have never quite seen before. Discover follows those possibilities; zoom brings the detail closer.

Space for curiosity

Seethe is built around looking, listening, and exploring. There is no account to create. You can pause, take your time, and share a flame when something catches your eye.

Made for the screen you are using

The native mobile experience includes Discover, zoom and pan, pause and refinement, ambient sound, and a local list of shared flames. The Mac version brings the same visual experience to a larger workspace. The Apple TV edition focuses on discovery and viewing with remote controls. Shared links also open in a lightweight web viewer.

Release preparation is underway. Features and availability differ between platforms. We will add public store links here when they are available.
